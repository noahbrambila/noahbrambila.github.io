Meet Noah Brambila

Noah is an 8th Grade student, regular Gardening Club attendee, and local library volunteer. As a student representative, he's fought to listen to the people who go to his school five days a week: the students. Noah's worked on the largest fundraiser in school history, pushed school pride with the 25th anniversary, and led the charge in bringing new ideas to the dances.

Now, Noah Brambila is running for Gardening Club President to do continue helping the people he represents.
