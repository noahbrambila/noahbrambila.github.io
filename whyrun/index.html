Why I'm Running...




Students want to join clubs that they feel support them.

Support can be through activities, community service hours, or the general club experience.

I’ve spent my time in school supporting students, and it’s time we take that support to a club level.

Our club has the foundations to succeed, and that’s helped us retain members every meeting. There never has been a meet where I feel like our club is too small, rather, I find myself wondering why we can't be big. Executives in years past have done what they’re supposed to do and have stayed in line with other clubs of our size. What’s become apparent to me, though, is that executives can do even more.

Executives can ensure us fun fundraisers.

Executives can ensure we earn community service hours with friends.

Executives can ensure we listen to our own club members.

So why don’t we have an executive that does this? Not because it's impossible, but because no one has dared to thrive. That changes this school year, and that’s the reason I am running for the club that so many members make a part of their Tuesday and Thursday afternoons. To those who love our club, to those who love their friends, and to those who love our school:

It's Time to Thrive.
